# Pricing & business model

Field-tested numbers for selling AI property-tour videos (Israeli market, produced from photos).

## Per-video (realistic)
| tier | who | price |
|---|---|---|
| single host / private Airbnb | one-off | ₪300–700 |
| volume (property manager, many units) | bulk | ₪150–350 / video |
| flythrough only, no VO | budget | ₪150–300 |

The money is in **volume + speed**, not a high per-unit price. Hard cost per video is low
(~₪25–50 in credits + your time), so even ₪300 leaves a healthy margin.

## Where the real money is
Not one-off sales — **property managers / hosting companies with 10–50 units**. One client = many
videos, and a recurring relationship (seasonal refreshes, new listings, social cuts).

## Worked deal — Ella Sun → Yonatan (10 villas)
- Each villa: **long (3 min) + short (1.5 min)**.
- Per villa: short ₪250 + long ₪350 = **₪600**.
- 10 villas = **₪6,000 total**, billed **per villa** (₪600 on delivery of each; first villa paid up front to start).
- Framed as a **launch / volume price** to protect future rates.

### Margin
| line | amount |
|---|---|
| revenue | ₪6,000 |
| Higgsfield (Ultra month + credits ~4,000) | ~₪500–700 |
| **profit (before your time)** | **~₪5,300–5,500 (~90%)** |
| your time | ~20–25 active hours for all 20 videos |

## Negotiation notes
- Don't discount for free — meet in the middle (₪6,500) or attach a condition (bigger first payment, launch-rate framing).
- Present it as **one package / solution**, not 20 line items — the per-video split is for your eyes.
- Include 1–2 revisions per video; charge beyond that.
- Upsell later: vertical 9:16 social cuts (+₪100/video), seasonal refresh retainer, new properties at the same volume rate.
